# SWYNEX Task 3: Food Waste Analytics Dashboard

## 1. The Problem
Food waste is not just about food being thrown away — it also means wasted money, resources, and effort. The goal of this project was to understand how food waste changes over time and identify the categories and operational patterns that deserve attention.

## 2. Evidence from the Data
Using **911 records** in Power BI, I analysed food waste, meals served, waste categories, and day-wise patterns.

- **40.85K kg** of total food waste recorded.
- **44.84 kg** average food waste per record.
- **341.99K meals** served across the dataset.
- Meat recorded the highest total waste at approximately **17.5K kg**.

## 3. Investigation
I built an interactive Power BI dashboard to explore waste trends over time, compare waste across categories, and examine differences between days and special-event records. Category and staff-experience slicers allow users to investigate specific parts of the dataset.

## 4. Key Insights
- **Meat is the biggest waste category:** It contributes the highest total waste, making it a priority for further investigation.
- **Waste changes over time:** The trend contains noticeable spikes, suggesting that some periods need closer examination.
- **Waste varies by day:** Average waste differs across day codes, which may help identify recurring operational patterns.
- **Meal volume matters:** The analysis indicates that serving more meals is strongly associated with higher food waste.

## 5. Recommended Business Actions
- **Improve meal planning:** Use expected attendance and historical meal demand to prepare more appropriate quantities.
- **Review meat preparation and storage:** Investigate purchasing, portion sizes, storage, and leftover handling.
- **Investigate waste spikes:** Compare unusual periods with events, meal volumes, and kitchen operations to identify possible causes.
- **Track performance regularly:** Monitor total waste, average waste, and waste per meal to measure whether improvements are working.

## Tools Used
**Power BI | Data Cleaning | Exploratory Data Analysis | DAX | Data Visualization**

## Project Outcome
This project turns raw food-waste records into a practical dashboard that helps stakeholders identify waste hotspots, explore operational patterns, and make more informed decisions about food preparation and resource usage.
